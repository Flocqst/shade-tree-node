Privacy is the shape

The view forgets on purpose.

Identity No onion, key, wallet, operator, or stable visual position enters the snapshot.

Place No IP, ASN, city, coordinate, or geographic pin is collected or inferred.

Activity No destinations, traffic volumes, tunnel counts, users, or agents are measured.

Time Observations are rounded to the publication cadence; only a bounded count history remains.

How this view is made

A census, outside the path.

The bootnode helps clients discover nodes; it never carries agent traffic. A scheduled observer reaches it over Tor, verifies a fresh signed directory, discards every entry, then signs the aggregate. This page verifies that dedicated publication signature before drawing.

Future shared stats

Small groves stay quiet.

Nodes do not send public usage telemetry today. If coarse, opt-in totals are added later, the public view will wait for at least five reporting nodes, delay the result by six hours, and round it heavily. That threshold would not prove five independent operators. Until then, silence is the privacy feature.
