what this means, and honest scope

The gate holds on every axis tested. Membership is necessary and sufficient to egress; the trusted-root check defeats invented sets; the nullifier budget throttles per member without identity; and junk is cheap to reject (a failed proof costs the gateway a verify, never an egress).

On "getting banned": there is nothing to ban. The gateway is reached by Tor rendezvous, so it never learns a client IP. The only lever against a member is its per-epoch budget, and that touches only that one nullifier.

Scope kept honest: H4 is a functional flood (25 concurrent), not a saturation DoS. Against real volume the outer wall is Tor's onion proof-of-work defense (this box runs a tor with pow: yes), which raises the client's cost to even reach the rendezvous, before the zk gate ever runs.
